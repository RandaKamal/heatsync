HeatSync

Intelligent 3D monitoring and control for mobile heating infrastructure — built for LB Energy's IHL platform.

Real-time data 3D visualization Anomaly detection Energy savings IMBAT Orion Multi-site
Core Features
Everything at a glance

A single dashboard surface that replaces spreadsheets, manual walkthroughs, and disconnected alarms.

🏗

Interactive 3D Campus

Full 3D model of the building campus — orbit, zoom, and fly inside any room. Each hall and its rooftop heat pump units are individually clickable.

🌡

Live Temperature Monitoring

Per-room supply and return air temperatures update in real time from the IHL backend. Floor-color heatmap provides instant spatial overview.

⚡

Power Draw Tracking

Current vs. rated power displayed per unit. A red progress bar warns when a unit exceeds 90 % capacity — before it becomes a fault.

📈

24-Hour Temperature History

Line chart shows room temperature, setpoint, and outside temperature over the past 24 hours, making deviation trends immediately visible.

💡

Energy Savings vs Baseline

7-day bar chart compares "with IHL" vs. "without IHL" consumption. Savings cards show kWh, kg CO₂, and EUR saved — updated daily.

🔔

Anomaly Detection Log

Decoded from Modbus error registers 1901–1904. Each anomaly shows type, severity, timestamp, and whether it is active or resolved.

Rooftop Units
IMBAT Orion — modelled to spec

Each unit in the 3D scene matches the real IMBAT Orion dimensions and behaviour, including animated axial fans and status-coded LEDs.

Unit status system

Every unit carries one of four operational states, shown by LED color, fan speed, and glow intensity in the 3D view.

Active Idle Warning Error

Supported models

Three Orion variants are tracked, covering the full range of heating capacities used across sites.

ORIO-025 — 25 kW, 1,250 m³/h ORIO-039 — 39 kW, 2,500 m³/h ORIO-047 — 47 kW, 4,000 m³/h

Click-to-inspect

Click any rooftop unit to open its detail panel: temperature gauge, power bar, history charts, anomaly list, and manual controls for setpoint and HVAC mode.

Zoom inside rooms

Scroll the camera all the way inside a hall to inspect the interior: ceiling LED strips, air-supply diffusers, and the wall-mounted control panel all render correctly from inside.

Visual Modes
Day · Evening · Night

A one-click toggle in the top-right of the 3D view cycles through three scene presets — useful for demos and presentations at any time of day.

☀

Day

Blue sky, bright sunlight, hard shadows. Grass and building colours show in full fidelity. Ideal for walkthroughs and client demos.

🌆

Evening

Low orange sun, warm ambient glow. Window panes emit a soft interior light. Good for sunset-hour status reviews.

🌙

Night

Dark blue sky, stars, cool moonlight. Building windows glow warm amber. Interior point lights visible when zoomed inside halls.

Backend Integration
Live data + fallback simulation

The frontend polls the IHL backend every 5 seconds. If the backend is unreachable it falls back to a realistic drift simulation — the UI never shows stale or frozen data.
